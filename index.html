A continuous, gently sloping parcel situated along the western ridge of the plateau, offering unobstructed views over the valley and direct private road access.

The land features mature native oak stands along its northern boundary, acting as a natural windbreak, while the central clearings remain bathed in sunlight throughout the day. It has remained fallow and free of chemical intervention for over three decades, preserving natural soil biome and drainage capacity.

Topography & Terrain

The site naturally splits into three natural terraces. The upper tier provides optimal structural ground for construction on bedrock, while the lower two tiers retain deep topsoil suitable for private orchards, gardens, or unbuilt recreation buffer zones.

“An undisturbed pocket of land where architectural privacy meets total climatic exposure.”
